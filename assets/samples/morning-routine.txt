I wake up at seven o'clock every morning. First, I drink a glass of water. Then I open the window and look outside.

After that, I make a cup of tea. While the tea is hot, I read the news on my phone. I read about technology and science. I like to learn something new every day.

At half past seven, I have breakfast. I usually eat bread with an egg. Sometimes I eat fruit too.

Then I get ready for the day. I wash my face, brush my teeth, and get dressed. I put my phone, keys, and wallet in my bag.

Before I leave, I make a list of things I want to do. The list helps me remember my goals. A good morning makes the whole day better.
