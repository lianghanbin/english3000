Coffee is one of the most popular drinks in the world. People drink it in the morning to wake up, and some people drink it all day long.

The coffee bean comes from a plant. Farmers pick the beans, dry them, and then roast them. Roasting gives the beans their brown color and strong smell.

There are many ways to make coffee. You can make it in a machine, with a simple pot, or even with a small paper filter. Some people like black coffee. Others add milk and sugar.

Coffee has caffeine. Caffeine is a natural thing that makes you feel awake and helps you focus. But too much coffee can make you nervous, and it can keep you awake at night.

Drinking coffee is also a social activity. Friends meet in cafes, talk, and drink coffee together. It is a simple drink, but it brings people together.
